Prices and limits

FreeUnlimited Apple Intelligence on the device (supported devices and languages); a few cloud messages a day with the free models; one image a day; web searches with a daily limit
Omnimo ProEvery model; hundreds of messages a day, with a smaller daily allowance for the flagships; long documents read in one go; image and video generation with credits included. $6.99 a week (3-day free trial), $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (1-week free trial). US prices; the app shows the price for your country.
CreditsFor images and videos. 300 for $4.99, 700 for $9.99, 1600 for $19.99; bought credits never expire.
Trials3 days on the weekly plan, 1 week on the yearly plan
Customers from before OmnimoPeople who bought privateSLM as a paid app, or a model pack, get Omnimo Pro at no charge until 31 October 2027.

Exact daily numbers are set on Omnimo's server and can change without an app update; the app shows what is left. Prices in other countries differ, and the app shows the local price.

Privacy

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Chat history is kept on the device. Chats with Apple Intelligence never leave it.
  • A message to a cloud model goes through Omnimo's server to the company that makes the model, to get the answer. Omnimo does not keep chats.
  • A photo goes to the model answering, without its location. Documents are read on the device; their text goes to the model picked.
  • The description of an image or video goes to fal.ai, which runs the model.
  • Usage statistics help improve the app and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Policy: privacy policy.
