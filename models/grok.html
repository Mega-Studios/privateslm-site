Good for

  • Another maker's take on a question you have asked GPT or Claude.
  • Everyday questions and writing in Grok's style, without a separate subscription.

How the allowance works. Free users get a few cloud messages a day with the free models, and unlimited Apple Intelligence on supported devices. Omnimo Pro has hundreds of messages a day for the free and Pro models, and a smaller daily allowance for the flagships. The exact numbers are set on Omnimo's server and the app shows how many are left.

In countries where some makers' models are not offered, these models are still available.

Try it

Open Omnimo, tap the model name at the top of the chat and pick it. You can switch in the middle of a chat; each answer shows which model wrote it. Ready prompts: the prompt library.

Questions

Can I use Grok without an X subscription?

In Omnimo, yes: Grok 4.7 and 4.3 are part of Omnimo Pro. xAI's own Grok app and X subscription have features Omnimo doesn't, such as Grok Imagine.
