Apple Intelligence in Omnimo

Updated November 25, 2026

Apple Intelligence includes a language model that runs on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Apple lets apps use it through its Foundation Models framework, and Omnimo does: chats with Apple Intelligence are free, unlimited, work offline and never leave the device.

Apple Intelligence in Omnimo
PlanFree, unlimited, on every plan
RunsOn your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
NeedsiOS, iPadOS or macOS 26 on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on, in a language it supports
InternetNot needed
When it is usedPick it in the model menu. A new user on a supported device starts with it. When the free cloud messages run out, or the internet is gone, the chat can carry on with it.

What to expect

Apple's on-device model is much smaller than the cloud flagships. It is good at short, everyday tasks: rewriting a message, a summary, a quick answer, a translation. For long documents, hard reasoning or photos, switch to a cloud model in the same chat.

Siri and Shortcuts in Omnimo, Ask, Summarize, Rephrase and Translate, run on the device with Apple Intelligence too.

Which devices

Apple keeps the list of devices that support Apple Intelligence: Apple Intelligence requirements. On other devices Omnimo works with the cloud models only. More: free offline AI on iPhone.

Questions

Is Apple Intelligence in Omnimo really unlimited?

Yes. It runs on your device, so it costs nothing to run and has no daily limit.

Does Apple Intelligence in Omnimo work offline?

Yes, on supported devices and languages. The cloud models need the internet.
