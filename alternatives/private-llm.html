Alternatives to Private LLM

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

Private LLM runs open models entirely on your iPhone, iPad or Mac for $4.99, once. If you want the same thing, offline and private, or something lighter, here are the options. One note first: Omnimo was privateSLM, an offline app like Private LLM, until October 2026. It is now a cloud AI app with Apple Intelligence on the device, so it is an alternative only if Apple's on-device model is enough for you.

The options

  1. Locally AI by LM Studio (Element Labs): best for free open models on iPhone and iPad. Runs Llama, Gemma, Qwen and others with Apple MLX; no login. Price (US): free download; no in-app purchases listed.
  2. PocketPal AI (Gooya): best for choosing any GGUF model. Search Hugging Face from the app and run what you download. Price (US): free download; PocketPal Plus from $3.99 (in-app purchase list).
  3. Omnimo (made by the team behind this site): Apple Intelligence free, unlimited and offline, with nothing to download, on iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26 on a supported device. Everything else in Omnimo is cloud models, which need the internet. Price (US): free; Pro for the cloud models from $6.99 a week.
  4. AllChat (IQ Digital): best for cloud and on-device in one app. Its listing offers on-device models for offline use next to cloud models. Price (US): Premium $7.99 weekly or $99.99 yearly, Pro $8.99 a month or $89.99 a year, Max $17.99 a month or $179.99 a year (in-app purchase list).

Ratings, prices and features: each app's US App Store listing and list of in-app purchases, read 25 November 2026. The App Store lists in-app purchases without their billing period; where a listing doesn't say, neither do we.

How we chose

We list apps we have looked at closely, with what their own listings say. We make Omnimo, so it is on every list; we have tried to say plainly when another app is the better choice.

Questions

Is Omnimo still an offline app?

Only partly. Apple Intelligence runs on your device and works offline, on devices and languages that support it. The downloadable open models of privateSLM were retired in October 2026; the other models in Omnimo are cloud models.

What is the best free offline AI app for iPhone?

If your iPhone has Apple Intelligence, Apple's model is already on it, and apps like Omnimo use it with nothing to download. For open models, Locally AI and PocketPal AI are free downloads. See free offline AI on iPhone.
