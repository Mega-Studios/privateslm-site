Alternatives to Poe

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

Poe's appeal is choice: many models and bots in one app. If you want that with a different pricing model, a native Mac app or an on-device model, these are the alternatives worth a look.

The options

  1. ChatNow (Tappz): best for the most models, including video. More than 30 models and side-by-side answers; needs iOS 26. Price (US): AI Plus $6.99, ChatNow Plus $19.99, yearly plans $49.99 to $79.99, credit packs (in-app purchase list); supports Family Sharing; needs iOS 26.
  2. Fello AI (AI Apps Brewery): best for work on Mac and iPhone. Creates Office files, keeps Projects and syncs between devices. Price (US): weekly $4.99, monthly $11.99, quarterly $24.99, yearly $89.99 (in-app purchase list); a free tier with limited daily messages.
  3. Omnimo (made by the team behind this site): best for several makers' models in one chat. GPT-6, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more, switchable mid-chat, with Apple Intelligence free on the device and no account. Price (US): free to start; $6.99 a week (3-day free trial), $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (1-week free trial). US prices; the app shows the price for your country.
  4. Perplexity (Perplexity AI): best for research. Answers with sources, with models from several makers behind it. Price (US): Perplexity Pro $20.00 (also listed at $200.00), Perplexity Max $200.00, credit packs from $5.00 (in-app purchase list).
  5. TypingMind (TypingMind): best for your own API keys. A web interface you buy once; you pay the providers directly. Price (US): a lifetime license, paid once (price on typingmind.com); you also pay the AI providers for what you use, with your own API keys.

Ratings, prices and features: each app's US App Store listing and list of in-app purchases, read 25 November 2026. The App Store lists in-app purchases without their billing period; where a listing doesn't say, neither do we.

How we chose

We list apps we have looked at closely, with what their own listings say. We make Omnimo, so it is on every list; we have tried to say plainly when another app is the better choice.

Questions

Is there a Poe alternative without points?

Omnimo counts messages a day instead of points: a few on the free plan, hundreds on Pro. TypingMind has no usage limits of its own: you pay the providers for what you use.

Which Poe alternative works offline?

Omnimo's Apple Intelligence works offline on supported devices. AllChat's listing also offers on-device models. Apps such as Locally AI and PocketPal run open models fully offline.
