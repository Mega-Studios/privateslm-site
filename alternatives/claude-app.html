Alternatives to the Claude app

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

Anthropic's Claude app is the most direct way to use Claude, and Claude Pro is $20 a month. Some people want Claude next to other models, a lower price, or Claude without an account. These are the options.

The options

  1. Omnimo (made by the team behind this site): Claude next to other makers' models. Claude Haiku 5.5 is free (a few messages a day); Claude Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 are in Omnimo Pro, next to GPT-6, Gemini and Grok. No account. Price (US): free to start; $6.99 a week (3-day free trial), $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (1-week free trial). US prices; the app shows the price for your country. See Claude in Omnimo.
  2. Poe (Quora): best for Claude among many bots. Poe offers Anthropic's models among many others. Price (US): Basic $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year, Plus $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year (in-app purchase list); usage is counted in points.
  3. ChatGPT (OpenAI): best for OpenAI's models. The most used assistant, with voice and memory. Price (US): ChatGPT Go $8.00, ChatGPT Plus $19.99, ChatGPT Pro from $100 (in-app purchase list); Plus is $20 a month on OpenAI's site (3 November 2026).
  4. Google Gemini (Google): best for Google's apps. Gemini with Gmail, Calendar and Photos. Price (US): Google AI Plus $4.99, Google AI Pro $19.99, Google AI Ultra $199.99 (in-app purchase list; the plans include Google storage).
  5. Perplexity (Perplexity AI): best for research with sources. Citations on every answer. Price (US): Perplexity Pro $20.00 (also listed at $200.00), Perplexity Max $200.00, credit packs from $5.00 (in-app purchase list).

Ratings, prices and features: each app's US App Store listing and list of in-app purchases, read 25 November 2026. The App Store lists in-app purchases without their billing period; where a listing doesn't say, neither do we.

How we chose

We list apps we have looked at closely, with what their own listings say. We make Omnimo, so it is on every list; we have tried to say plainly when another app is the better choice.

Questions

Can I use Claude without the Claude app?

Yes. Apps such as Omnimo and Poe offer Anthropic's Claude models; Omnimo uses Anthropic's API. Features of Anthropic's own app, such as projects and connectors, are only in the Claude app.

Is Claude free in Omnimo?

Claude Haiku 5.5 is one of Omnimo's free cloud models, with a few messages a day. Sonnet, Opus and Fable are part of Omnimo Pro.
