Alternatives to ChatOn

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

ChatOn is a popular all-in-one AI app. If you want a different set of models, a monthly plan or an on-device model, here are the alternatives, with what their own listings say.

The options

  1. ChatGPT (OpenAI): best for OpenAI's own app. The original, with a generous free tier and OpenAI's newest features. Price (US): ChatGPT Go $8.00, ChatGPT Plus $19.99, ChatGPT Pro from $100 (in-app purchase list); Plus is $20 a month on OpenAI's site (3 November 2026).
  2. Genie (Chatbot AI Assistant) (AppNation): best for research and images. Deep Search reports, image editing and voice chat. Price (US): Weekly Unlimited $7.99, other weekly plans $8.99 to $12.99, yearly $39.99 and $99.99 (in-app purchase list); supports Family Sharing.
  3. Nova (ScaleUp): best for a guided app. Scan-and-solve, Word and Excel files, voice mode. Price (US): 1 Week Chatbot Pro $4.99 to $7.99, Nova Premium $9.99, 1 Year Chatbot Pro $39.99 and $59.99 (in-app purchase list).
  4. ChatNow (Tappz): best for the most models. More than 30 models including video. Price (US): AI Plus $6.99, ChatNow Plus $19.99, yearly plans $49.99 to $79.99, credit packs (in-app purchase list); supports Family Sharing; needs iOS 26.
  5. Omnimo (made by the team behind this site): best for several makers' models in one chat. GPT-6, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more, switchable mid-chat, with Apple Intelligence free on the device and no account. Price (US): free to start; $6.99 a week (3-day free trial), $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (1-week free trial). US prices; the app shows the price for your country.

Ratings, prices and features: each app's US App Store listing and list of in-app purchases, read 25 November 2026. The App Store lists in-app purchases without their billing period; where a listing doesn't say, neither do we.

How we chose

We list apps we have looked at closely, with what their own listings say. We make Omnimo, so it is on every list; we have tried to say plainly when another app is the better choice.

Questions

Is there a ChatOn alternative with a monthly plan?

Yes. Omnimo Pro has a monthly plan at $9.99 in the US, besides $6.99 a week and $39.99 a year. ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity also sell monthly plans.

Which ChatOn alternative lets me choose the model?

Omnimo, ChatNow, Poe and Fello AI all let you pick the model by name.
