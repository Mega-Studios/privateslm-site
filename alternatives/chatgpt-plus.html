Alternatives to ChatGPT Plus

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

ChatGPT Plus is $19.99 a month in the App Store ($20 on OpenAI's site). Most people looking for an alternative want one of three things: a cheaper plan, a different maker's model, or several makers' models for one price. Here are the honest options.

The options

  1. ChatGPT (OpenAI): best for staying with OpenAI for less. ChatGPT Go, at $8.00 in the App Store's list, is OpenAI's own cheaper plan: the simplest switch if you like ChatGPT and want to pay less. Price (US): ChatGPT Go $8.00, ChatGPT Plus $19.99, ChatGPT Pro from $100 (in-app purchase list); Plus is $20 a month on OpenAI's site (3 November 2026).
  2. Claude by Anthropic (Anthropic): best for writing, coding and long thinking. Anthropic's own app, with Claude's newest models first and connectors to Google Drive, Gmail and Calendar. Price (US): Claude Pro $20.00 a month or $214.99 a year, Claude Max $124.99 a month, Claude Ultra $249.99 a month (in-app purchase list).
  3. Google Gemini (Google): best for people who live in Google's apps. Gemini connects to Gmail, Calendar, Photos and YouTube, and Google AI Pro includes Google storage. Price (US): Google AI Plus $4.99, Google AI Pro $19.99, Google AI Ultra $199.99 (in-app purchase list; the plans include Google storage).
  4. Perplexity (Perplexity AI): best for research with sources. Every answer comes with citations, and Pro adds deeper research tools. Price (US): Perplexity Pro $20.00 (also listed at $200.00), Perplexity Max $200.00, credit packs from $5.00 (in-app purchase list).
  5. Poe (Quora): best for the widest choice. Many models and a million custom bots, from $4.99 a month. Price (US): Basic $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year, Plus $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year (in-app purchase list); usage is counted in points.
  6. Omnimo (made by the team behind this site): best for several makers' models in one chat. GPT-6, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more, switchable mid-chat, with Apple Intelligence free on the device and no account. Price (US): free to start; $6.99 a week (3-day free trial), $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (1-week free trial). US prices; the app shows the price for your country.

Ratings, prices and features: each app's US App Store listing and list of in-app purchases, read 25 November 2026. The App Store lists in-app purchases without their billing period; where a listing doesn't say, neither do we.

How we chose

We list apps we have looked at closely, with what their own listings say. We make Omnimo, so it is on every list; we have tried to say plainly when another app is the better choice.

Questions

What is the cheapest alternative to ChatGPT Plus?

Among the options here, Poe Basic is $4.99 a month and ChatGPT Go $8.00 in the US App Store (25 November 2026). Omnimo is free to start, with unlimited Apple Intelligence on supported devices, and Omnimo Pro is $9.99 a month.

Is there an app with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini in one subscription?

Yes, several: Omnimo, Poe, ChatNow and Fello AI among them. Omnimo offers OpenAI's GPT-6, Anthropic's Claude and Google's Gemini: the makers' models, used through pay-per-token APIs, not the ChatGPT app.
