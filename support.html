Support

Help with the Omnimo app (formerly privateSLM) for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Contact us

Write to team@getmegabrain.com. Tell us your device, the app's version (Settings → About) and what happened.

Restore purchases

Open Settings → About → Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought with, and Pro comes back on that device. Credits you bought are kept with your App Store account for this app, so they come back on their own.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open Settings → Manage subscription in the app, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge; Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too: ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Credits

Credits pay for images, videos and photo filters. Pro includes credits each period, and credits you buy never expire, with or without Pro. Your balance is in Settings → Credits.

Privacy and terms

What leaves your device and where it goes: Privacy Policy. Purchases are covered by Apple's standard licence agreement.
