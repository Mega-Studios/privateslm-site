The best AI chat apps for iPhone in 2026

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

There is no single best AI chat app: the right one depends on which models you want, how you pay, and whether you need it to work offline. This list says what each app is best for and what to watch out for. It is written by the team that makes Omnimo, which is on the list, so we have tried to be especially fair to the others.

The list

  1. ChatGPT: best for most people, and anyone who wants OpenAI's newest features first

    The most used AI app, with a generous free tier, voice conversations, image generation and history on every device. Watch out: OpenAI's models only.

    ChatGPT Go $8.00, ChatGPT Plus $19.99, ChatGPT Pro from $100 (in-app purchase list); Plus is $20 a month on OpenAI's site (3 November 2026). Rated 4.8 from 12.5 million ratings.

  2. Claude by Anthropic: best for writing, coding and thinking a problem through

    Anthropic's own app, with the newest Claude models first, research with citations and connectors to Google apps. Watch out: Anthropic's models only; Claude Pro is $20 a month.

    Claude Pro $20.00 a month or $214.99 a year, Claude Max $124.99 a month, Claude Ultra $249.99 a month (in-app purchase list). Rated 4.7 from 315,000 ratings.

  3. Google Gemini: best for people who live in Google's apps

    Gemini Live with the camera, Nano Banana image editing, and Gmail, Calendar and Photos in the chat. Watch out: Google's models only; some features need a Google AI plan.

    Google AI Plus $4.99, Google AI Pro $19.99, Google AI Ultra $199.99 (in-app purchase list; the plans include Google storage). Rated 4.7 from 2.46 million ratings.

  4. Perplexity: best for research with sources

    Every answer cites its sources; Pro Search and Deep Research go further. Watch out: Built around search more than chat.

    Perplexity Pro $20.00 (also listed at $200.00), Perplexity Max $200.00, credit packs from $5.00 (in-app purchase list). Rated 4.8 from 530,000 ratings.

  5. Omnimo: best for several makers' models in one chat, plus a free on-device model

    GPT-6, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more, switchable mid-chat; unlimited Apple Intelligence on supported devices; documents read on the device; no account. Pro is $9.99 a month. Watch out: New, with very few ratings; fewer models than ChatNow or Poe; no sync between devices.

    Free to start; Pro $6.99 a week (3-day free trial), $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (1-week free trial). US prices; the app shows the price for your country. One rating on the US App Store when we checked. We make Omnimo.

  6. Poe: best for the widest choice of models and bots

    Many makers' models, image and video generators, and a million custom bots; several bots in one chat. Watch out: Usage is counted in points, so how far a plan goes depends on the models you use.

    Basic $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year, Plus $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year (in-app purchase list); usage is counted in points. Rated 4.7 from 53,800 ratings.

  7. ChatNow: best for the most models in one subscription, video included

    More than 30 models, side-by-side answers and video models like Veo 3.1 and Sora 2. Watch out: Needs iOS 26.

    AI Plus $6.99, ChatNow Plus $19.99, yearly plans $49.99 to $79.99, credit packs (in-app purchase list); supports Family Sharing; needs iOS 26. Rated 4.8 from 148,000 ratings.

  8. Fello AI: best for work across Mac, iPhone and iPad

    Creates real PowerPoint, Excel, Word and PDF files, keeps Projects and syncs between devices. Watch out: The yearly plan ($89.99) costs more than most.

    Weekly $4.99, monthly $11.99, quarterly $24.99, yearly $89.99 (in-app purchase list); a free tier with limited daily messages. Rated 4.5 from 144 ratings.

  9. Grok: best for xAI's models and Grok Imagine

    xAI's own app: web research, documents and photos, and image and video generation. Watch out: xAI's models only.

    SuperGrok Lite $10, SuperGrok $30, SuperGrok Plus $100, SuperGrok Heavy $300 (in-app purchase list). Rated 4.9 from 1.63 million ratings.

  10. Locally AI by LM Studio: best for fully offline, free open models

    Runs Llama, Gemma, Qwen and others on the device with Apple MLX; no login, no data collection. Watch out: Small open models are far less capable than the cloud flagships; iPhone and iPad.

    Free download; no in-app purchases listed. Rated 4.7 from 2,047 ratings.

Also popular

ChatOn, Genie and Nova are all-in-one AI apps with large followings, ready prompts, image tools and weekly plans; their listings name models from several makers. Private LLM ($4.99 once) and PocketPal AI run open models fully offline. Comparisons: Omnimo vs other apps.

How we made this list

  • Prices are each app's US App Store list of in-app purchases, and ratings its US App Store rating, read on 25 November 2026. Apple lists in-app purchases without the billing period; where a listing doesn't say, neither do we.
  • Features are what each app's own listing says, plus our own use of the apps.
  • We ran no benchmarks, and the order is not a score: each app is placed by what it is best for.
  • We make Omnimo. Nobody paid to be on this list.

Questions

What is the best AI chat app for iPhone?

For most people, ChatGPT. For writing and coding, Claude; for Google's apps, Gemini; for research with sources, Perplexity; for several makers' models in one app, Omnimo, Poe, ChatNow or Fello AI; for free offline AI, an app that uses Apple Intelligence, or Locally AI.

Is there a free AI chat app that works offline on iPhone?

Yes. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, apps such as Omnimo use Apple's on-device model, free and offline. Locally AI and PocketPal AI run open models offline on any recent iPhone.
