Private AI for statements and reports — on your iPhone and Mac

October 24, 2026

Short answer: you can ask an AI about a bank statement, an invoice or an annual report without the file leaving your device. privateSLM reads PDF, Excel, Word and scanned pages on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and answers with a model that runs there — no account, no upload, and it works in airplane mode.

What you can ask

  • "List every payment over $500 on this statement."
  • "What are the three largest expenses this quarter?"
  • "Summarise this annual report: revenue, profit, cash, risks."
  • "Which invoices are past due?"
  • "Explain this tax letter in plain English."

Limits worth knowing

  • Small models are better at finding and explaining than at adding up long columns. Check totals against the document.
  • Nothing here is financial or tax advice.

FAQ

Can AI read my bank statement without uploading it?

Yes, with a model that runs on your device. privateSLM opens the PDF, spreadsheet or scan on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and answers there; the file is never sent to a server, and it works offline.

Which finance AI models run offline on iPhone and Mac?

privateSLM's catalog has Finance LLM 7B and Finance Chat 7B, each a 4.08 GB download that needs a device with at least 8 GB of RAM — a recent Pro iPhone, an M-series iPad or any Apple Silicon Mac.

Is it financial advice?

No. The models summarise, extract and explain; they can make mistakes, especially with arithmetic. Check totals against the document.

Does it read Excel files?

Yes. privateSLM reads PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages and text files, and scanned pages, on the device.
