Private AI for contracts — on your iPhone and Mac

October 24, 2026

Short answer: an AI can read a contract without the contract leaving your device. privateSLM opens a PDF, a Word file or a photo of a page on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and answers with a language model that runs there too — no account, no server, no upload, and it keeps working in airplane mode.

Cloud assistants work by sending what you type, and what you attach, to the provider's servers. For a client's agreement, a lease under negotiation or anything under an NDA, that is often the one thing you cannot do. An on-device model has nowhere to send it.

What you can ask

  • "Summarise this agreement in ten lines."
  • "What is the notice period, and how is notice given?"
  • "List every obligation of the tenant."
  • "Is there a limitation of liability? Quote it."
  • "Explain the termination clause in plain English."

Scanned pages are read with on-device text recognition. Long documents work too: privateSLM finds the passages that answer the question, or reads the document part by part for a summary.

What it costs

privateSLM is free: chat, documents and voice with Apple Intelligence or the free general models. The legal models are part of the app's paid expert-model upgrade; the current price is on the App Store page.

Limits worth knowing

  • These are 7-billion-parameter models, not a lawyer: they can miss a clause or misread one. Treat answers as a fast first read and check them against the text.
  • They are general legal models, not trained on any one jurisdiction's case law.
  • Nothing here is legal advice.

FAQ

Can I use AI on a confidential contract without uploading it?

Yes, if the model runs on your own device. privateSLM reads the PDF, Word file or scan on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and answers with a model that runs there too, so the document is never sent to a server. It works in airplane mode.

Which legal AI models run offline on iPhone and Mac?

privateSLM's catalog has two legal models: SaulLM 7B (Legal), a 4.37 GB download, and Law Chat 7B, a 4.08 GB download. Both need a device with at least 8 GB of RAM, such as a recent Pro iPhone, an M-series iPad or any Apple Silicon Mac.

Is the answer legal advice?

No. The models are assistants for reading and drafting: they summarise, find clauses and explain terms, and they can be wrong. Check anything that matters against the document itself.

Do the legal models cost money?

privateSLM is free to download, and chatting about documents with Apple Intelligence or the free general models costs nothing. The legal models are part of the app's paid expert-model upgrade.
