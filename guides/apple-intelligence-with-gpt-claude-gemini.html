Apple Intelligence and GPT, Claude and Gemini in one chat

Updated November 25, 2026

Apple Intelligence has a model that runs on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. It is free, private and works offline, but it is small. GPT, Claude and Gemini are much larger and run in the cloud. Omnimo puts both in one chat: start with Apple Intelligence, switch to GPT-6, Claude or Gemini when a question needs more, and switch back.

How it works in Omnimo

Apple IntelligenceGPT-6, Claude, Gemini and the rest
Where it runsOn your deviceIn the cloud, through Omnimo's server to the model's maker
PriceFree, unlimitedA few messages a day free (GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, DeepSeek V4 Flash and Qwen 3.8 Flash); every model on Omnimo Pro
InternetNot neededNeeded
Your messagesNever leave the deviceSent to the model's maker to get the answer; Omnimo doesn't keep them
Good forShort everyday tasks: rewriting, summaries, quick answers, translationLong documents, photos, hard questions, careful writing

In the same chat: when the free cloud messages run out, or the internet goes, the conversation can carry on with Apple Intelligence. Siri and Shortcuts (Ask, Summarize, Rephrase, Translate) use Apple Intelligence on the device.

What you need

Apple Intelligence in Omnimo needs iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26 on a device that supports it. Apple's list (Apple Intelligence requirements, read November 25, 2026): iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone Air and iPhone Duo; iPad mini with A17 Pro or A20 Pro and iPads with M1 or later; Macs with M1 or later and MacBook Neo. It also has to be turned on in Settings, and works in the languages Apple supports. On other devices, Omnimo works with the cloud models only.

Other ways to get both

  • Apple's own ChatGPT extension. On supported devices, Siri and Writing Tools can hand a request to ChatGPT, if you turn it on in Settings. It is built into the system, but it is ChatGPT only, and not a chat app.
  • AllChat: its listing offers its own on-device models next to cloud models.
  • Two apps. An offline app such as Locally AI next to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

Questions

Is there an app that uses Apple Intelligence and ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

Yes. Omnimo has Apple Intelligence on the device and the GPT-6, Claude and Gemini models in the cloud, in one chat. (It uses OpenAI's GPT models, not the ChatGPT app.)

Can I switch from Apple Intelligence to Claude in the middle of a chat?

Yes. Pick another model from the menu at the top of the chat and ask again.
