Free offline AI on iPhone

Updated November 25, 2026

Most AI apps need the internet: the model runs on a server. Two kinds of AI run on the iPhone itself, for free, and keep working in airplane mode: Apple Intelligence, already on supported iPhones, and small open models you download. Here is what each needs, and the honest catch.

1. Apple Intelligence: nothing to download

On a supported iPhone, Apple's on-device model is already there. Apps can use it through Apple's Foundation Models framework; Omnimo does, free and unlimited, and the chat never leaves the phone.

The catch: not every iPhone has it. Apple's list (Apple Intelligence requirements, read November 25, 2026): iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone Air and iPhone Duo; iPad mini with A17 Pro or A20 Pro and iPads with M1 or later; Macs with M1 or later and MacBook Neo. Omnimo needs iOS 26 for it, Apple Intelligence has to be turned on in Settings, and it works only in the languages Apple supports. On other iPhones Omnimo still works, but only with its cloud models, which need the internet.

2. Open models you download

Apps like Locally AI (free), PocketPal AI (free) and Private LLM ($4.99 once) download an open model, such as Llama, Gemma or Qwen, and run it on the phone. They work on more iPhones than Apple Intelligence does.

The catch: a model is a download of hundreds of megabytes to several gigabytes, larger models need more memory than many phones have, and long answers warm the phone and use battery.

What offline AI is good for, and what it isn't

Good forNot so good for
Rewriting, shortening and fixing a messageFacts it might not know, or recent news
Quick summaries and translationsLong documents and hard reasoning
Private notes you don't want to send anywherePhotos (Omnimo sends photo questions to a cloud model)
Planes, trains and bad signalAnything where a mistake is costly: check it

A practical setup is both: offline for the everyday, a cloud model for the hard questions. In Omnimo they are in the same chat, and when the free cloud messages run out or the signal drops, the chat carries on with Apple Intelligence.

Questions

Can I use AI on my iPhone without the internet?

Yes, in two ways: Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones, in apps such as Omnimo, and apps that download an open model, such as Locally AI or PocketPal AI.

Which iPhones can run Apple Intelligence?

According to Apple (November 25, 2026): iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone Air and iPhone Duo. In Omnimo it also needs iOS 26.
