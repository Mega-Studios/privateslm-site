Is ChatGPT down?

Updated November 25, 2026 · an explainer, not a live status page

This page can't tell you whether ChatGPT is down right now: it is a static page. The answer is on OpenAI's official status page:

  • status.openai.com

OpenAI's status page shows ChatGPT, the API and OpenAI's other services separately, with current incidents and their history.

If the status page says everything is fine

Then the problem is probably closer to you. In order, try:

  1. Another network: switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or turn off a VPN.
  2. Signing out and in again, or closing and reopening the ChatGPT app.
  3. Updating the app, or trying ChatGPT on the web from another device.
  4. A shorter message: a very long conversation or file can fail when a short one works.
  5. Waiting a few minutes: many problems are short and don't reach the status page.

If ChatGPT is down

An outage usually affects one maker at a time. Another maker's model can carry on in the meantime: in Omnimo you can switch from GPT-6 to Claude, Gemini, Grok or DeepSeek in the same chat, and on supported devices Apple Intelligence answers on the device with no internet at all.

To be clear about what this does and doesn't fix: Omnimo reaches OpenAI's models through an API. When OpenAI's API is down, GPT-6 in Omnimo can be down too; the other makers' models are not affected. When only the ChatGPT app or website is down, the API often still works. GPT-6 in Omnimo.

Questions

How do I know if ChatGPT is down?

Check OpenAI's official status page, listed on this page. It shows current incidents for ChatGPT and its API.

What can I use while ChatGPT is down?

Another maker's model. In Omnimo you can switch to Claude, Gemini, Grok or DeepSeek in the same chat; on a device with Apple Intelligence, it answers on the device without the internet.
