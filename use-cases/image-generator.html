Make images and short videos with AI

Updated November 25, 2026

Describe what you want and get it in the chat: an illustration, a photo-style image, a short video clip. Photo filters restyle a photo you choose.

How to do it in Omnimo

  1. Open Explore and pick Generate image or Create video, or Photo filters to restyle a photo.
  2. Describe it: subject, style, light, framing. A watercolor of a lighthouse at dawn, soft light.
  3. Not right? Change one thing at a time and try again.

Which model

Images and videos are paid in credits, not messages. One image a day is free. Omnimo Pro includes credits every period, and credit packs (300 for $4.99, 700 for $9.99, 1600 for $19.99 in the US) never expire. Each image or video model costs a set number of credits, shown in the app.

Where your data goes

The description you write, and a photo you restyle, go to fal.ai, which runs the image and video models. Privacy policy.

Prompts to start with

  • A clean product photo
  • An illustration in a style
  • Logo ideas

Questions

Is the image generator free?

One image a day is free. More images, and video, use credits, which Pro includes and anyone can buy.

Can it make videos?

Yes, short clips, as part of Pro and paid in credits.
