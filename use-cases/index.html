Use cases

Updated November 25, 2026

The things people most often open Omnimo for, and how to do each one.

Use case

Summarize a PDF on iPhone, iPad or Mac

Attach a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or Pages file, or a scan, and ask Omnimo for a summary, the key dates, a clause or the numbers. The file is read on your device.
Use case

Homework help with AI: photo of the problem, step-by-step answer

Take a photo of a math problem or a worksheet and ask Omnimo to explain it step by step, with GPT-6, Claude, Gemini and more. Free to start.
Use case

Translate with AI on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Translate text, a photo of a sign or menu, or a whole document with GPT-6, Claude, Gemini and more in Omnimo. Apple Intelligence translates on the device, free and offline.
Use case

Write emails with AI on iPhone and Mac

Draft an email, reply to one, or make yours shorter, warmer or more formal with GPT-6, Claude, Gemini and more in Omnimo. Rephrase on the device with Siri and Shortcuts.
Use case

Ask AI about a photo

Take or choose a photo and ask Omnimo: what is this, what does it say, how do I fix it. GPT-6, Claude, Gemini and more can see the photo; its location is not sent.
Use case

Make images and short videos with AI

Describe an image or a short video and get it right in the chat in Omnimo: an illustration, a photo-style image or a short clip. One image a day is free; video is part of Pro.
