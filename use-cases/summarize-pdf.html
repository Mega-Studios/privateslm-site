Summarize a PDF on iPhone, iPad or Mac

Updated November 25, 2026

Omnimo reads documents on your device and answers questions about them: a summary, the key dates, one clause, the numbers in a table. It works with PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Pages files and with scans.

How to do it in Omnimo

  1. Tap the attachment button in a chat and choose the file, or scan a page with the camera.
  2. Ask: Summarize this in five bullet points, or something narrower: What does it say about cancellation?
  3. Ask follow-up questions in the same chat. Switch to a flagship model for a long or difficult document.

Which model

Any model reads the text. For long documents, Omnimo Pro reads them in one go, and flagships such as Claude Sonnet 5.5 or GPT-6 Sol are a good choice for careful reading. If a document is too long to read in one go, the app says which pages the answer covers.

Where your data goes

The document is turned into text on your device. That text goes to the model you picked, through Omnimo's server, to get the answer; Omnimo does not keep it. With Apple Intelligence nothing leaves the device. Privacy policy.

Prompts to start with

  • Summarize a document
  • Find the red flags in a contract
  • Pull out every date and deadline

Questions

Can Omnimo summarize a scanned PDF?

Yes. Scans and photos of pages are read on your device and the text goes to the model.

Is there a page limit?

The limit is the model's longest prompt in Omnimo: about 64K tokens for the free and Pro models, less for some flagships. On Pro a long document is read in one go; a very long prompt counts as more than one request.
