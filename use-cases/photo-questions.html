Ask AI about a photo

Updated November 25, 2026

Show Omnimo what is in front of you and ask about it: a plant, a label, an error on a screen, a broken part, a chart, a page of handwriting.

How to do it in Omnimo

  1. Tap the camera or photo button in a chat and take or choose a photo.
  2. Ask: What is this?, What does it say?, How do I fix it?
  3. Follow up in the same chat; Omnimo sends the newest photos with your questions.

Which model

Most models in Omnimo can see photos, including the free GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite and Qwen 3.8 Flash. Three can't (DeepSeek V4 Flash, DeepSeek V4 Pro and GLM 5.3); with a photo in the chat, Omnimo switches to one that can and says so.

Where your data goes

The photo goes to the model answering, without its location and other metadata, through Omnimo's server, which doesn't keep it. Privacy policy.

Prompts to start with

  • What is this?
  • How do I fix it?
  • Read a label and explain it

Questions

Is asking about a photo free?

Yes, within the free plan's few cloud messages a day.

Does Apple Intelligence see photos in Omnimo?

No; photo questions go to a cloud model that can see them.
