Homework help with AI: photo of the problem, step-by-step answer

Updated November 25, 2026

Take a photo of the problem, or type it, and ask for an explanation you can follow. The point is to understand it: ask for the steps, a hint, or a similar problem to try yourself.

How to do it in Omnimo

  1. Take or choose a photo of the problem. The photo goes to the model without its location.
  2. Ask: Explain how to solve this step by step. Don't just give the answer.
  3. Stuck on one step? Ask about that step. Want practice? Give me a similar problem.

Which model

Photo questions need a model that sees photos: most do, including the free GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku 5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite. For hard math, try a flagship and compare with a second model in the same chat: models can make mistakes, and a disagreement is a reason to check.

Where your data goes

The photo goes to the model answering, without its location, through Omnimo's server, which doesn't keep it. Privacy policy.

Prompts to start with

  • Explain a problem step by step
  • Quiz me on a topic
  • Explain it simply

Questions

Is Omnimo's homework help free?

Yes, to start: a few cloud messages a day are free, photo questions included. Omnimo Pro has hundreds a day.

Can I trust the answer?

Check it. AI can make mistakes, especially in long calculations. Asking a second model the same question in the same chat is a quick way to catch one.
