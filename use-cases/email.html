Write emails with AI on iPhone and Mac

Updated November 25, 2026

Tell Omnimo who the email is for and what it needs to say, or paste the email you got, and get a draft to edit. Set the tone of replies in any chat, or make an assistant that always writes the way you do.

How to do it in Omnimo

  1. Paste the email you are answering, or describe the one you need.
  2. Ask: Reply politely, decline the meeting, suggest next week.
  3. Refine: Shorter, Warmer, More formal. Or select text in any app and use Rephrase, which runs on the device.

Which model

Any model writes a good short email; the free ones are fine. For a sensitive message, try a flagship like Claude Sonnet 5.5 and compare with GPT-6 Sol in the same chat.

Where your data goes

Pasted text goes to the model you picked through Omnimo's server, and is not kept. Rephrase in Siri and Shortcuts uses Apple Intelligence on the device. Privacy policy.

Prompts to start with

  • Decline politely
  • Follow up without being pushy
  • Reply to a complaint

Questions

Can Omnimo send the email for me?

No. Omnimo writes the draft; you copy it into your mail app and send it.

Can it write in my style?

Make an assistant with instructions about your style, or paste an email you wrote and ask it to match.
