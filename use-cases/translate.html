Translate with AI on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Updated November 25, 2026

Paste text, take a photo of a sign or a menu, or attach a document, and ask for a translation in the tone you need: formal, casual, or word for word with notes. Omnimo speaks 46 languages.

How to do it in Omnimo

  1. Paste the text, or attach a photo or file.
  2. Ask: Translate into Spanish, keep it formal, or Translate and explain any idioms.
  3. For a quick translation from any app, use the Translate shortcut in Siri and Shortcuts, which runs on the device.

Which model

Apple Intelligence translates free and offline on supported devices and languages. For long or nuanced texts, a cloud model like Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6 Sol or Mistral Large 4 is a good choice.

Where your data goes

With Apple Intelligence the text stays on the device. With a cloud model it goes through Omnimo's server to the model's maker to get the answer, and is not kept. Privacy policy.

Prompts to start with

  • Translate and keep the tone
  • Translate a sign or menu from a photo
  • Practice a conversation

Questions

Does Omnimo translate offline?

With Apple Intelligence, yes, on supported devices and in the languages it supports. Cloud models need the internet.

Which languages?

The app is in 46 languages, and the cloud models translate between many more.
