Questions

Is privateSLM free?

Yes. Chat with Apple Intelligence or the free general models (Llama, Qwen, Gemma, Phi and others), documents, voice, chat history, Siri and Shortcuts and the widget cost nothing. There are no ads, no account and no subscription. Only the specialist models are paid, in one-time packs.

What do the expert packs cost?

Each pack is a one-time purchase of $4.99 in the US: the Tech Pack (coding, SQL, math and security models), the Business Pack (law, finance and translation) and the Health Pack (medical and mental health). All Packs is $9.99 once and includes every pack added later. Purchases work with Family Sharing.

I bought privateSLM when it was a paid app. Do I have to pay again?

No. Until 10 October 2026 privateSLM was a paid download; since 11 October it is free, and everyone who bought it before gets every pack.

Does privateSLM work offline?

Yes. The model runs on your device, so chat, documents and voice keep working in airplane mode. The internet is only needed to download a model, once.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, and Mac with macOS 14 or later — one download covers all three. On devices with Apple Intelligence (iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26) it answers with Apple's on-device model and nothing to download; on other devices you download a free model once, from 0.7 GB to 2.5 GB.

Does privateSLM send my chats or documents anywhere?

No. There is no server component: no account, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking. Conversations and documents stay on the device.

Is privateSLM the same app as Private LLM?

No. Private LLM is a different app from a different developer. Both run AI on-device with no subscription; the comparison page explains where each one is stronger.
