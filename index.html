Questions

Is Omnimo free?

Yes, it is free to download and to start. Apple Intelligence on your device is free and unlimited on supported devices and languages, and you get a few messages a day with cloud models (GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku, Gemini Flash-Lite, DeepSeek Flash and Qwen Flash) and one image a day. Omnimo Pro unlocks every model, hundreds of messages a day, and image and video generation. In the US it is $6.99 a week (with a 3-day free trial), $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year (with a 1-week free trial); the app shows the price for your country.

Which AI models are in Omnimo?

GPT-6 (Luna, Sol and Astra), Claude (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus and Fable), Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GLM and MiniMax, plus Apple Intelligence on your device. You can switch models at any time, even in the middle of a chat. Which models are offered can depend on your country.

How is Omnimo different from ChatGPT Plus?

ChatGPT Plus is OpenAI's own subscription and gives you OpenAI's models. Omnimo is an independent app with models from several makers — OpenAI's GPT-6, Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini, xAI's Grok, DeepSeek and others — in one chat, and you can switch between them mid-conversation. ChatGPT Plus was $20 a month in the US when we checked on 3 November 2026; Omnimo Pro is $9.99 a month. Omnimo is not affiliated with OpenAI or any other model maker.

Does Omnimo work offline?

Partly. Apple Intelligence runs on your device and works offline, on devices that support it (iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26 on an Apple Intelligence device) and in its supported languages. The cloud models — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and the rest — need an internet connection.

Where are my chats stored? Is it private?

Your chat history is kept on your device. Chats with Apple Intelligence never leave it. When you pick a cloud model, your messages go through our server to the company that makes that model, to get the answer; we don't keep your chats. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Usage statistics help us improve the app, and you can turn them off in Settings. The privacy policy has the details.

Is Omnimo the same app as privateSLM?

Yes. privateSLM was renamed Omnimo in October 2026: the same App Store app (id 6504996891), now with cloud AI models alongside Apple Intelligence. The downloadable offline models and the one-time model packs were retired. People who bought privateSLM as a paid app or bought a model pack get Omnimo Pro at no charge until 31 October 2027 — what changed. Omnimo is not related to Omni AI, Omnia or OMNI AI.

Which devices does Omnimo run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, and Mac with macOS 14 or later. One App Store app covers all three, and a Pro subscription works on each. Apple Intelligence needs iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26 on a device that supports it.
