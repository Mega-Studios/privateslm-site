privateSLM is now Omnimo

November 24, 2026 · 3 min read

Omnimo is the new name of the app that was called privateSLM. It is the same App Store app (id 6504996891), for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and an update is all it takes. What changed is what it does: Omnimo puts the leading AI models in one chat, and still starts free.

What Omnimo is

  • The leading models in one app. GPT-6 (Luna, Sol and Astra), Claude (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus and Fable), Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GLM and MiniMax. Switch at any time, even in the middle of a chat. Which models are offered can depend on your country.
  • Apple Intelligence, still free. On supported devices and languages, Apple's on-device model answers without limits, works offline, and nothing leaves your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  • More than chat. Show it a photo, ask your documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, scans — read on the device), get answers with sources from the web, and create images and short videos right in the chat.
  • What you already used. Voice in and out, chat history, Siri and Shortcuts, the Home Screen widget — and now your own assistants and the tone of replies, in 46 languages.

What it costs

Free to download and to start: unlimited Apple Intelligence, a few messages a day with the free cloud models (GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku, Gemini Flash-Lite, DeepSeek Flash, Qwen Flash) and one image a day. Omnimo Pro unlocks every model, flagships included, hundreds of messages a day, long documents in one go, and image and video generation with credits included. In the US it is $6.99 a week (with a 3-day free trial), $9.99 a month, or $39.99 a year (with a 1-week free trial); the app shows the price for your country. Credits for more images and videos are $4.99, $9.99 and $19.99, and bought credits never expire.

What went away

privateSLM ran open models such as Llama, Qwen and Gemma on the device through llama.cpp, and sold expert model packs once. Those downloadable models and the packs are gone: the model pages on this site now lead here. Apple Intelligence stayed, so a fully on-device chat is still one tap away.

If you used privateSLM

  • You don't pay again. If you bought privateSLM when it was a paid app, or bought any model pack, you have Omnimo Pro at no charge until 31 October 2027. The app recognises your purchase on its own; if Pro doesn't show, open Settings → About → Restore Purchases.
  • Where your messages go has changed — and you choose. privateSLM sent nothing anywhere. In Omnimo, chats with Apple Intelligence still stay on your device. When you pick a cloud model, your message goes through our server to the company that makes it, to get the answer; we don't keep your chats. There is still no account, no ads and no tracking, and the usage statistics can be turned off in Settings. The privacy policy has every detail.

Not to be confused with

Omnimo is not related to Omni AI, Omnia or OMNI AI, and it is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Alibaba, Zhipu or MiniMax: their model names belong to them. The posts on this blog from before the rename, about small models running on the device, stay up as an archive.
