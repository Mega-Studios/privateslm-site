llama.cpp climbed 72 releases since our last edition. Our catalog didn't grow by one model.

July 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Between our last edition (July 13, tag b9982) and today (July 17, tag b10054), llama.cpp's build counter climbed 72 releases. None of them explain why privateSLM's catalog is exactly the same size it was last week. The reason is upstream of the engine entirely: nothing new and phone-sized showed up anywhere we looked.

What we swept

Hugging Face's GGUF listings sorted by both "trending" and "created," bartowski's and Unsloth's recent uploads, lmstudio-community and ggml-org's repos, and vendor pages for Qwen, Gemma, Phi, Mistral, Liquid AI, and Apple. Our bar: chat/instruct, ≤2.5 GB at Q4_K_M, released or newly quantized in the last 14 days, reputable quantizer, a license that permits end-user download. Zero releases cleared all five today. The trending GGUF lists were dominated by finetunes and abliterated merges of models we've already assessed, or MoEs (GLM-5.2 at 754B total, Qwen3.6 at up to 397B) miles outside a phone budget regardless of architecture.

What llama.cpp actually shipped in those 72 releases

Backend and hardware work, not new model families: a Hexagon (Qualcomm) L2 cache rework, SYCL fused top-k MoE kernels, KleidiAI SME/SME2 dispatch on Arm, a Vulkan transfer-queue synchronization fix, CUDA graph support for Volta-generation GPUs, and tensor-parallel fixes for Phi-3, BERT and PLaMo. Useful if you're running on one of those specific backends. No new LLM_ARCH_* entries relevant to a phone-sized chat model landed in this window — the architecture gate we've written about before simply didn't come up today, because nothing needed it.

One release worth flagging, even though it doesn't qualify

IBM shipped Granite 4.1 on April 29: dense 3B, 8B and 30B variants, Apache 2.0, and per IBM's own post the 8B instruct model "consistently matches or outperforms the Granite 4.0 32B Mixture-of-Experts model." That's a real, sourced generational jump over the Granite 3.1 8B already sitting in our catalog. It's 79 days old today, well past our 14-day freshness window for a "new drop," so it isn't today's addition — but it's a plausible deliberate-upgrade candidate worth evaluating for the catalog on its own merits, not smuggled in under a "new this week" label it doesn't earn.

The closest thing to model news this week

PrismML released a ternary-quantized build of Qwen3.6-27B on July 14 that claims to squeeze a 27-billion-parameter model into single-digit gigabytes, including a tier marketed explicitly for the iPhone 17 Pro. It's not vaporware — the ternary GGUF alone has over 74,000 downloads — but neither the advertised file size nor the "runs on your phone" framing survives a direct check against the actual download and a stock copy of llama.cpp. We ran that check by hand: what the file actually weighs, and whether anything you can install without cloning a fork can open it. Today's deep-dive has the numbers.

Today's catalog, unchanged

Zero additions, zero removals from this run. The 21-model catalog carries over exactly as it was, plus Apple Intelligence on devices that have it. A quiet week for the catalog is what "nothing cleared the bar" is supposed to look like — not a gap in the check, the check working.
