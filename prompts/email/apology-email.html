Apologise for a mistake

Prompt library · Email

Own it, fix it, move on.

The prompt

Write an apology email for [what happened]. Say what went wrong in one sentence, take responsibility without over-explaining, say what I'm doing to fix it and to stop it happening again: [steps]. Under 120 words.

Tips

  • Ask it to remove anything that sounds like an excuse.

Use it in Omnimo

Open a new chat in Omnimo, paste the prompt, replace the parts in [brackets] and send. The free models handle this well; for something long or important, switch to a flagship and compare.
