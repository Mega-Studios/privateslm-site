Reply to a complaint

Prompt library · Email

Calm, specific, and fair.

The prompt

Write a reply to this customer complaint. Acknowledge the problem in their words, apologise for what we got wrong (not for what we didn't), say exactly what we will do and by when: [your fix]. No jargon, no excuses.

Complaint:
[paste it]

Tips

  • Say what you can and can't offer.
  • Ask it to check the tone is not defensive.

Use it in Omnimo

Open a new chat in Omnimo, paste the prompt, replace the parts in [brackets] and send. The free models handle this well; for something long or important, switch to a flagship and compare.
