Ask for a raise or a review

Prompt library · Email

Confident, with the facts up front.

The prompt

Help me write an email to my manager asking for a meeting about my pay. Facts: [results, new responsibilities, market data]. Keep it short and confident, ask for a meeting rather than a number in the email, and suggest two times.

Tips

  • Ask it to play your manager and push back.
  • Keep the numbers for the meeting.

Use it in Omnimo

Open a new chat in Omnimo, paste the prompt, replace the parts in [brackets] and send. The free models handle this well; for something long or important, switch to a flagship and compare.
