The prompt

A simple flat logo for [name], a [business type]. A single [symbol idea] mark, two colours, [colours], on white, clean geometric shapes, no text.

Tips

  • Image models are poor at lettering: add the name yourself.
  • Treat it as a sketch, not a final logo.

Use it in Omnimo

In Omnimo, open Explore and pick Generate image, or ask in a chat, and paste the description. Images use credits: one image a day is free, Pro includes credits every period.
