Explain a problem step by step

Prompt library · Study

Understand it, don't just copy it.

The prompt

Explain how to solve this problem step by step. Number the steps, say why each one is done, and don't skip arithmetic. At the end, check the answer a second way.

Problem:
[type it, or attach a photo]

Tips

  • Attach a photo of the worksheet.
  • Ask a second model to check the answer.

Use it in Omnimo

In a chat in Omnimo, tap the camera or photo button and add the photo, then paste the prompt and fill in the [brackets]. The photo goes to a model that can see it, without its location.
