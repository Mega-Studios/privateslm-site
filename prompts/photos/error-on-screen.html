Explain an error on a screen

Prompt library · Photos

A screenshot to a fix.

The prompt

This error appeared on my [device or app]. What does it mean, what usually causes it, and what should I try, in order from simplest to most involved?

[attach a screenshot or photo]

Tips

  • Say what you did just before it appeared.

Use it in Omnimo

In a chat in Omnimo, tap the camera or photo button and add the photo, then paste the prompt and fill in the [brackets]. The photo goes to a model that can see it, without its location.
