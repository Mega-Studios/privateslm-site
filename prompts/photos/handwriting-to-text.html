Turn handwriting into text

Prompt library · Photos

Notes, letters and recipes, typed out.

The prompt

Type out the handwriting in this photo exactly. Mark any word you can't read as [?]. Then, separately, tidy it into clean notes.

[attach a photo]

Tips

  • Good light and a flat page help most.

Use it in Omnimo

In a chat in Omnimo, tap the camera or photo button and add the photo, then paste the prompt and fill in the [brackets]. The photo goes to a model that can see it, without its location.
