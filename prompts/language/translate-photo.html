Translate a sign or menu from a photo

Prompt library · Languages

Travel without a phrasebook.

The prompt

Translate everything in this photo into [language]. For a menu, explain any dish I might not know and mark what contains [meat / nuts / pork].

[attach a photo]

Tips

  • Ask how to pronounce what you want to order.

Use it in Omnimo

In a chat in Omnimo, tap the camera or photo button and add the photo, then paste the prompt and fill in the [brackets]. The photo goes to a model that can see it, without its location.
