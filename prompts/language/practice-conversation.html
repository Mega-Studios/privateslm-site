Practice a conversation

Prompt library · Languages

A patient partner who corrects you.

The prompt

Let's practise [language] at [level]. You are [a waiter / a colleague / a shop assistant] in [situation]. Speak only [language], keep your lines short, and after each of my replies, correct my mistakes in one line in English before you answer.

Tips

  • Use voice input to practise speaking.
  • Turn on spoken answers to practise listening.

Use it in Omnimo

Open a new chat in Omnimo, paste the prompt, replace the parts in [brackets] and send. The free models handle this well; for something long or important, switch to a flagship and compare.
