Where Nova is better

Nova has a polished onboarding, scan-and-solve for homework, Word and Excel files, voice mode and a long track record. Its listing says it supports more than 140 languages.

Where Omnimo is better

Omnimo lets you choose the model by name and switch mid-chat, has Apple Intelligence free and offline on supported devices, reads documents on the device, needs no account and has a Mac app.

The honest verdict

Choose Nova if you like a guided app with scan-and-solve and don't mind not choosing the model.

Choose Omnimo if you want to pick the model yourself and have a free on-device model.

Both are free to try: Nova on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where Nova wins.

Questions

Which models does Nova use?

Its listing names GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.6 Flash, Claude Opus 5, Kimi K3, Grok 4.5 and DeepSeek V4 Pro.

How much is Nova?

Its in-app purchases include weekly plans from $4.99 to $7.99, Nova Premium at $9.99 and yearly plans at $39.99 and $59.99 (25 November 2026). Omnimo Pro is $6.99 a week, $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US.

Can Omnimo solve homework from a photo?

Yes: take or choose a photo and ask. The photo goes to the model answering, without its location. See homework help.
