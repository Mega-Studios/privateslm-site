Where AllChat is better

AllChat's Consensus Mode, which sends your question to several models and builds one answer, shows where they agreed and differed. It also has Smart Routing, alerts, memory, a ChatGPT memory import and its own on-device models for offline use.

Where Omnimo is better

Omnimo speaks 46 languages (AllChat's listing has English only), and its on-device model is Apple Intelligence, which needs no download. Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

The honest verdict

Choose AllChat if you want several models to check each other's answers.

Choose Omnimo if you want an app in your language and to pick the model yourself.

Both are free to try: AllChat on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where AllChat wins.

Questions

Does Omnimo combine answers from several models?

No. Omnimo answers with the model you choose; you can switch and ask again in the same chat.

Do both apps work offline?

Both have an on-device option. Omnimo uses Apple Intelligence on supported devices and languages; AllChat's listing says it offers on-device models for offline use.

How much is AllChat?

Its in-app purchases include Premium at $7.99 a week, Pro at $8.99 a month or $89.99 a year, and Max at $17.99 a month or $179.99 a year (25 November 2026). Omnimo Pro is $6.99 a week, $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US.
