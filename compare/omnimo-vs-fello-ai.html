Where Fello AI is better

Fello AI creates real PowerPoint, Excel, Word and PDF files from a chat, keeps work in Projects, syncs everything across Mac, iPhone and iPad, and has Deep Research and podcasts from documents. Its listing names more models, Gemini 4 among them.

Where Omnimo is better

Omnimo has Apple Intelligence free, unlimited and offline on supported devices, reads documents on the device and needs no account. Pro is cheaper: $9.99 a month and $39.99 a year, against $11.99 and $89.99 in Fello's list of in-app purchases.

The honest verdict

Choose Fello AI if you want files made for you, Projects and sync between Mac and iPhone.

Choose Omnimo if you want a free on-device model and a cheaper yearly plan, and don't need sync.

Both are free to try: Fello AI on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where Fello AI wins.

Questions

Does Omnimo sync chats between iPhone and Mac?

No. Omnimo keeps chat history on each device. Fello AI syncs across Mac, iPhone and iPad.

How much is Fello AI?

Its in-app purchases are $4.99 a week, $11.99 a month, $24.99 a quarter and $89.99 a year (25 November 2026), with a free tier of limited daily messages. Omnimo Pro is $6.99 a week, $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US.

Can Omnimo create a PowerPoint or Excel file?

No. Omnimo reads PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Pages files and answers questions about them, but it does not create Office files.
