Omnimo vs Poe

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

Poe, from Quora, was one of the first apps to put many AI models in one place, and Omnimo does the same thing on a smaller scale. The difference is mostly breadth against simplicity.

Where Poe is better

Poe has far more: models from many makers, image and video generators, and over a million custom bots, including ones you can build and share. You can bring several bots into one chat to compare answers. Its Basic plan starts at $4.99 a month, cheaper than Omnimo Pro.

Where Omnimo is better

Omnimo is simpler: one list of models, a daily allowance instead of points, and Apple Intelligence free and offline on supported devices. Documents are read on your device, there is no account, and Siri and Shortcuts work on the device. Pro at $9.99 a month includes every model in the app.

The honest verdict

Choose Poe if you want the widest choice of models and bots, or to build your own bots.

Choose Omnimo if you want a short list of leading models, a free on-device model and no account.

Both are free to try: Poe on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where Poe wins.

Questions

Does Omnimo use points like Poe?

No. Omnimo gives a number of messages a day: a few on the free plan, hundreds on Pro. Flagship models count against a smaller daily allowance.

Is Poe cheaper than Omnimo?

Poe Basic is $4.99 a month and Poe Plus $19.99 a month in the App Store's list (25 November 2026). Omnimo Pro is $9.99 a month, $6.99 a week or $39.99 a year in the US.

Can I make my own bot in Omnimo?

You can make your own assistants, with a name, a purpose, an icon and instructions, and pick the tone of replies. They stay on your device; Omnimo has no public bot store like Poe.
