Private LLM vs privateSLM: an honest comparison

Where Private LLM wins

It's cheaper. It has a far larger model catalog, including big 70B-class models if you own a Mac with serious RAM. It integrates with Siri and Shortcuts today, runs on Vision Pro, and it has three years of reputation behind it. If you specifically want uncensored model variants, that is their specialty and we don't compete there.

Where privateSLM wins

The first five minutes. On any device with Apple Intelligence, privateSLM answers immediately after install — the system model is already on your device, so there's nothing to download. Private LLM's own reviews show the cost of the other approach: multi-gigabyte downloads before the first conversation, and heavier models struggling on older phones. We also think a curated catalog is a feature, not a limitation — every model in privateSLM states its RAM needs up front, ships with a correct chat template, and is checked before it's listed (the process runs in public on our daily blog). Add built-in voice input and spoken replies, and full chat history with search.

The honest verdict

Choose Private LLM if you want the biggest possible model selection, uncensored variants, Siri/Shortcuts automation today, or 70B models on a big Mac.

Choose privateSLM if you want AI that works the moment it's installed, a specialist for a concrete job — code, math, medical or legal reading, finance, translation — without wading through 140 options, and voice conversations. Both respect your privacy; neither sends your chats anywhere.

FAQ

Is Private LLM or privateSLM better for offline use?

Both run entirely on-device and work in airplane mode. The difference is the first minutes: privateSLM can answer instantly on Apple Intelligence devices with zero downloads; Private LLM needs a multi-gigabyte model download before the first chat.

Which is cheaper?

Private LLM — $4.99 vs our $7.99, both one-time. If price is the deciding factor, they win that row.

Does either app send conversations to a server?

No. Both run inference on-device and keep conversations local. Neither requires an account.
