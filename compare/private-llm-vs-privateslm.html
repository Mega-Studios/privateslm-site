Where Private LLM wins

It has a far larger model catalog, including big 70B-class models if you own a Mac with serious RAM. Its Siri and Shortcuts work on older systems (ours need iOS or macOS 26), it installs on Intel Macs, and it has three years of reputation behind it. One $4.99 purchase covers that whole catalog, with no subscription, where our expert models need privateSLM Pro: a subscription, or $19.99 once for Lifetime. If you specifically want uncensored model variants, that is their specialty and we don't compete there.

Where privateSLM wins

The first five minutes. On any device with Apple Intelligence, privateSLM answers immediately after install — the system model is already on your device, so there's nothing to download. Private LLM's own reviews show the cost of the other approach: multi-gigabyte downloads before the first conversation, and heavier models struggling on older phones. We also think a curated catalog is a feature, not a limitation — every model in privateSLM states its RAM needs up front, ships with a correct chat template, and is checked before it's listed (the process runs in public on our daily blog). Add chat with your documents — PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages and scanned pages, read on the device, which Private LLM's FAQ says it does not do — built-in voice input and spoken replies, and chat history with search. And it costs nothing to start: chat with Apple Intelligence or the general models, documents and voice are free.

The honest verdict

Choose Private LLM if you want the biggest possible model selection, uncensored variants, Siri and Shortcuts before iOS 26, 70B models on a big Mac, or one $4.99 purchase that covers every model.

Choose privateSLM if you want AI that works the moment it's installed, a specialist for a concrete job — code, math, medical or legal reading, finance, translation — without wading through 140 options, questions about your own documents, and voice conversations. Both respect your privacy; neither sends your chats anywhere.

FAQ

Is Private LLM or privateSLM better for offline use?

Both run entirely on-device and work in airplane mode. The difference is the first minutes: privateSLM can answer instantly on Apple Intelligence devices with zero downloads; Private LLM needs a multi-gigabyte model download before the first chat.

Which is cheaper?

For everyday use, privateSLM: chat with Apple Intelligence or the general models, documents and voice are free, with no subscription. Its expert models come with privateSLM Pro: $9.99 a year with a 1-week free trial, $2.99 a month, or $19.99 once for Pro Lifetime. Private LLM is $4.99 once for the whole app, with no subscription, so for specialist models Private LLM costs less. Both cover iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Does either app send conversations to a server?

No. Both run inference on-device and keep conversations local. Neither requires an account.

Can Private LLM or privateSLM read PDFs?

privateSLM reads PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, text files and scanned pages on the device and answers questions about them. Private LLM's FAQ says it does not read documents or files.
