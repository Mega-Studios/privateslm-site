Omnimo vs ChatGPT

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

ChatGPT is OpenAI's own app, and with 12.5 million ratings on the US App Store it is the one most people start with. Omnimo is a smaller, independent app that puts models from several makers in one chat, OpenAI's GPT-6 among them. They answer different questions: "the best of OpenAI" or "the right model for each question".

Where ChatGPT is better

OpenAI's newest models and features usually reach ChatGPT first, and Omnimo does not have all of them: Advanced Voice Mode, memory across chats, history synced between your phone, computer and the web. Its free tier is generous, its image generator is built in, and it has a far bigger track record. If you only ever use GPT, ChatGPT is the better app.

Where Omnimo is better

You are not limited to one maker: ask GPT-6, then Claude or Gemini the same question in the same chat. On a device with Apple Intelligence, Omnimo also has a free, unlimited model that works offline and sends nothing anywhere. There is no account, and Pro is $9.99 a month against $19.99 for ChatGPT Plus in the App Store, for models from several makers.

The honest verdict

Choose ChatGPT if you want OpenAI's latest features first, voice conversations, memory and your chats on every device and the web.

Choose Omnimo if you want GPT, Claude and Gemini side by side for one subscription, a free on-device model, and no account.

Both are free to try: ChatGPT on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where ChatGPT wins.

Questions

Is Omnimo the same as ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT is OpenAI's own app. Omnimo is an independent app that offers OpenAI's GPT-6 models next to Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and others. It is not affiliated with OpenAI.

Is Omnimo cheaper than ChatGPT Plus?

Omnimo Pro is $9.99 a month in the US. ChatGPT Plus is $19.99 in the App Store's list of in-app purchases (25 November 2026) and $20 a month on OpenAI's site (3 November 2026); ChatGPT Go is $8.00. The plans are not the same thing: Plus gives more of OpenAI's models and features, Omnimo Pro gives several makers' models with a daily allowance.

Can I use GPT-6 for free in Omnimo?

GPT-6 Luna is one of Omnimo's free cloud models, with a few messages a day. GPT-6 Sol, Terra and Astra are part of Omnimo Pro.
