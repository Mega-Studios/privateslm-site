Omnimo vs Perplexity

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

Perplexity, like Omnimo, puts models from several makers behind one app. But Perplexity is built around search: every answer comes with sources. Omnimo is built around choosing the model, with web answers as one tool among several.

Where Perplexity is better

For research, Perplexity is the stronger app: cited sources on every answer, Pro Search and Deep Research, Labs for reports, and an assistant that can act for you. It has half a million ratings and apps on many platforms. Omnimo's Ask Web also answers with sources, but it is one tool with a daily limit, not the core of the app.

Where Omnimo is better

In Omnimo you choose the model, by name, and can switch in the middle of a chat. It reads your documents on the device, and it has Apple Intelligence free and offline on supported devices. Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year, against $20 a month for Perplexity Pro.

The honest verdict

Choose Perplexity if most of your questions are research and you want sources on every answer.

Choose Omnimo if you mostly chat, write and ask about photos and documents, and want to pick the model yourself.

Both are free to try: Perplexity on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where Perplexity wins.

Questions

Does Omnimo search the web like Perplexity?

Omnimo has Ask Web, which answers with numbered sources. It has a daily limit, higher on Pro. Perplexity is built around search and has deeper research tools.

Which is cheaper, Omnimo Pro or Perplexity Pro?

In the US, Omnimo Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Perplexity Pro is $20.00 in the App Store's list of in-app purchases (25 November 2026), which also lists it at $200.00.

Can I choose Claude or GPT in Omnimo?

Yes. Omnimo lists every model by name, GPT-6, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more, and you can switch at any time, even mid-chat.
