Omnimo vs TypingMind

Updated November 25, 2026 · prices from the US App Store

TypingMind and Omnimo solve the same problem, many models in one place, in opposite ways. TypingMind is software you buy once and connect to the AI providers with your own API keys. Omnimo includes the models: no keys, one subscription.

Where TypingMind is better

With TypingMind you pay once for the interface and then only for what you use, at the providers' own prices, with any model your keys reach. It stores data locally by default and has agents, plugins, prompts and a knowledge base. For a heavy or technical user it can be much more flexible.

Where Omnimo is better

Omnimo needs no API keys, accounts with providers or setup: install it and ask. It is a native iPhone, iPad and Mac app with a camera for photo questions, Siri and Shortcuts, a widget and Apple Intelligence free on the device. The price is fixed, with a free tier to start.

The honest verdict

Choose TypingMind if you already have API keys, use AI heavily, and want to pay providers directly.

Choose Omnimo if you want a phone app that works without keys or setup.

Both are free to try: TypingMind and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where TypingMind wins.

Questions

Do I need an API key for Omnimo?

No. The models are included. A free plan gives a few cloud messages a day and unlimited Apple Intelligence; Omnimo Pro gives every model.

Is TypingMind an iPhone app?

TypingMind is a web app that runs in the browser; it is not on the App Store.

Which is cheaper?

It depends on use. TypingMind is a one-time license plus what the providers charge for your usage. Omnimo Pro is $6.99 a week, $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US, models included.
