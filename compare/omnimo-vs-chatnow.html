Where ChatNow is better

ChatNow has more: over 30 models, video models like Veo 3.1 and Sora 2, a mode that asks several models the same question side by side, and a Playground that builds small apps. It supports Family Sharing and has a large base of reviews.

Where Omnimo is better

Omnimo runs on older systems (iOS 17 and macOS 14; ChatNow needs iOS 26), has Apple Intelligence free and offline on supported devices, reads documents on the device and needs no account. Its monthly Pro plan is $9.99.

The honest verdict

Choose ChatNow if you want the most models, video generation and side-by-side answers.

Choose Omnimo if you want a free on-device model, an app that runs on iOS 17, and a cheaper monthly plan.

Both are free to try: ChatNow on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where ChatNow wins.

Questions

Does ChatNow have more models than Omnimo?

Yes. ChatNow lists more than 30 models for chat, images and video. Omnimo has nine chat model families plus Apple Intelligence, and a smaller set of image and video models.

How much does ChatNow cost?

Its in-app purchases include AI Plus at $6.99, ChatNow Plus at $19.99 and yearly plans from $49.99 to $79.99 (25 November 2026). Omnimo Pro is $6.99 a week, $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US.

Can I compare two models in Omnimo?

Switch models in the same chat and ask again: each answer shows which model wrote it. Omnimo does not show several answers side by side.
