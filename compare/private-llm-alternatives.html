Why people look past Private LLM

Private LLM is the established name: on the App Store since 2023, about 740 US ratings, one $4.99 purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac with Family Sharing. The reasons people ask for an alternative are narrower: it is paid up front, its FAQ says it cannot read documents or files, and it does not mention Apple's built-in model, so the first conversation waits for a model download. If none of those matter to you, there is no reason to switch.

The alternatives, one line each

  • privateSLM — free; answers with Apple's model on iOS, iPadOS and macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, or a free model from 0.7 to 2.5 GB elsewhere; reads PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages and scanned pages on the device; voice in and out; 22 curated models with the RAM each needs stated. Expert models (code, law, finance, medicine and more) come with privateSLM Pro: a subscription with a free trial, or Lifetime once.
  • Locally AI — free, by LM Studio; MLX models plus Apple's Foundation Models, Siri, a local voice mode. Needs iOS 18.1, and macOS 26 on a Mac.
  • PocketPal AI — free and MIT-licensed; any GGUF from Hugging Face, also on Android. You choose quants and templates yourself.
  • Enclave — free for on-device models, with documents and voice; its paid plan is for cloud models. Needs iOS 27.
  • Noema — free; several engines including Apple's model, and documents. Newer, with few ratings so far.

FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Private LLM?

Locally AI and privateSLM are free and both use Apple's on-device model, so on a device with Apple Intelligence there is nothing to download; privateSLM also reads PDF and Office documents. PocketPal AI is free and open source if you want to run any GGUF model from Hugging Face.

Is there a Private LLM alternative that reads PDFs?

Yes. privateSLM reads PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, text files and scanned pages on the device; Enclave and Noema read PDFs too. Private LLM's FAQ says it does not read documents or files.

Do these alternatives work offline?

Yes. All of them run the model on the device, so once a model is downloaded — or with Apple's built-in model — they work in airplane mode. Enclave's paid plan is the exception: it is for cloud models.

Which one has the most models?

Private LLM, with 142, including 70B-class models on a Mac with enough memory. PocketPal AI can load any GGUF from Hugging Face. privateSLM lists 22, each checked before it is listed.
