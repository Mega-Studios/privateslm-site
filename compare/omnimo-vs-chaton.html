Where ChatOn is better

ChatOn has a large library of ready prompts (100+), YouTube video summaries, an AI keyboard, and image and video generation from photos. It has years of reviews behind it and is rated 4.7.

Where Omnimo is better

Omnimo names every model and lets you pick: GPT-6, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more, switchable mid-chat. Apple Intelligence on the device is free and unlimited, and documents are read on the device. Pro has a monthly plan at $9.99 besides the weekly and yearly ones.

The honest verdict

Choose ChatOn if you want ready prompts, YouTube summaries and an AI keyboard.

Choose Omnimo if you want to choose the model yourself, a free on-device model and a monthly plan.

Both are free to try: ChatOn on the App Store and Omnimo on the App Store. Omnimo is made by the team that writes this site, so weigh this page accordingly; we have tried to say where ChatOn wins.

Questions

Which models does ChatOn use?

Its App Store listing says it is built on GPT-5, Gemini 3.5, Claude Sonnet 5 and Sonar. Omnimo lists GPT-6, Claude (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, Fable), Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GLM and MiniMax.

How much is ChatOn compared with Omnimo?

ChatOn's in-app purchases include Premium at $6.99 and $7.99 a week and at $29.99 and $39.99 (25 November 2026). Omnimo Pro is $6.99 a week, $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US.
