Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · applies to the privateSLM app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and to this website

In one line: privateSLM does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer has no server that your chats or documents could reach.

What stays on your device

Your conversations, the documents you attach, the text extracted from them, summaries and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Answers are produced by a language model running on the device — Apple's on-device model where Apple Intelligence is available, or an open model you downloaded. Spoken replies and chat translation also run on the device.

When the app uses the internet

  • Model catalog. The app downloads the list of available models from www.privateslm.dev. The request carries nothing about you.
  • Model downloads. Models you choose are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which receives the request like any download.
  • Purchases. Buying, restoring and managing purchases and subscriptions goes through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; the developer receives no personal or payment information.
  • Voice input. Dictation uses Apple's Speech framework. Where your language can be recognised on the device, the app requires that; for languages iOS cannot recognise locally, the audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Links you open — this website, the App Store, Hugging Face model pages — open in your browser.

What the developer receives

Only what Apple provides to every developer in App Store Connect: aggregated download, sales and usage figures, and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers. None of it includes your conversations or documents.

This website

www.privateslm.dev uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: github.com/Mega-Studios/privateslm/discussions.
