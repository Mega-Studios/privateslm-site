Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026 · applies to the Omnimo app (formerly privateSLM) for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and to this website

Omnimo is made by Mega Studios ("we"). This policy explains what happens to what you type, attach and create in the app.

Apple Intelligence: nothing leaves your device

When you chat with Apple Intelligence, your messages and documents are processed on your iPhone, iPad or Mac by Apple's on-device model. We receive nothing.

Cloud models: your messages go to the model's maker, through our server

The welcome screen says this when you first open the app, and by continuing you accept it. When you chat with a cloud model (for example GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or DeepSeek), which is what the app starts with:

  • What is sent: the conversation you are having with that model — your messages, the model's earlier replies in that chat, your system prompt or your assistant's instructions, the tone you picked, the text of any document you attached to the chat, and any photo you chose to ask about. Documents are read on your device; only their text is sent, never the file.
  • Where it goes: to our server, which forwards it to our AI routing service and from there to the company that makes the model you picked: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Alibaba (Qwen), Moonshot (Kimi), MiniMax, Zhipu (GLM), Mistral, Meta (Llama) or Xiaomi (MiMo), in some cases through OpenRouter. What each of them does with it is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.
  • What we keep: no chat content. Our server keeps, per App Store account, a count of the day's messages, which it needs to apply the daily limits, and for a subscription, which App Store accounts have used it. Our routing service keeps request metadata (model, size, time, cost) for billing and troubleshooting and is set not to store message content.
  • Identifier: to know whose daily limit a message counts against, the app sends the App Store's signed record of your download and purchases. It contains an App Store identifier for your download of this app — not your name, email address or Apple Account.

Photos in a chat: your photo goes to the model's maker

When you choose or take a photo to ask about, a copy reduced to at most 1536 pixels and without its metadata (such as where it was taken) goes through our server to the company that makes the model answering, as described above. The camera is used only when you press Take a photo. We keep no photos: our server passes the photo on without storing it. The copy stays on your device with the chat, and goes when you delete the chat.

Images and videos: your description goes to fal.ai

When you ask for an image or a video, the description you type goes through our server to fal.ai, which runs the model you picked, from Alibaba, Black Forest Labs, OpenAI, ByteDance, Recraft, Google, xAI, MiniMax, Lightricks or Kuaishou. What each of them does with it is governed by its own terms. We keep no descriptions and no files: our server keeps your balance of credits, the day's count and, for a couple of days, a record of the job and the temporary link to its file. The finished file is downloaded to your device and stored there.

Photo filters: your photo goes to fal.ai

When you apply a filter or describe an edit, a copy of the photo you chose, reduced to at most 1536 pixels and without its metadata, goes through our server to fal.ai, with what you asked for, which runs the model you picked (FLUX.2 from Black Forest Labs or Nano Banana from Google). What each of them does with it is governed by its own terms. We keep no photos: our server passes the photo on without storing it, and keeps the day's count, whether you have used your free filter and, for a couple of days, a record of the job and the temporary link to its result. The result is downloaded to your device and stored there.

Web search: your question goes to Parallel

When Ask Web is on, the question you asked, and the one before it in that chat so a follow-up makes sense, goes through our server to Parallel Web Systems, a search service, and the pages it finds go to the model you picked, as described above. What Parallel does with it is governed by its own terms. We keep no questions: our server keeps a count of the day's searches. When Ask Web is off, nothing is searched.

Your assistants

The assistants you make stay on your device. A chat with one sends the model its name, purpose and instructions with each message, as described under cloud models.

Chats, documents and settings

Your chat history, created images and videos, photos you edited, attached documents and settings are stored on your device only. Deleting a chat or the app deletes them. There is no account and no copy on our side.

Purchases and credits

Subscriptions and credits are sold by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchases are recorded with RevenueCat, our subscription provider, under the same App Store identifier, so that Pro works on your devices and renewals, refunds and restores are handled correctly. Your balance of credits, and an identifier of each purchase of them, are kept on our server against that identifier, so that a reinstall keeps them.

Usage statistics

The app sends Amplitude statistics about how it is used, so we can see what works: which screens were opened, which features and models were used and how often, how long replies took, how purchases went, your device type, language and storefront, the App Store identifier for your download and a random identifier the statistics library makes for this installation. They never include the text of your messages, replies, descriptions, searches, photos or documents, the names of your files, the pages you open, or your IP address. Turn them off at any time in Settings → Analytics.

Voice

Dictation uses Apple's Speech framework, on the device where your language allows it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Replies read aloud are spoken on the device.

No tracking

Omnimo has no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites. It never asks for the advertising identifier.

This website

www.privateslm.dev uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

Children

Omnimo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: team@getmegabrain.com.
